FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still wondering? Text or call 1-365-357-3006, or DM @revmedia.ca on Instagram.

Q1Can a real website be built in 24 hours?

Yes. This is a focused one-page starter website built from a clear intake process. It is designed for speed, clarity and lead generation.

Q2What do I need to provide?

Your business name, service details, contact information, preferred style, your logo if you have one, and any photos or content you want included.

Q3What if I do not have a logo?

That is included. We create a clean starter logo as part of the $499 package.

Q4Is this custom or template-based?

It should feel custom to your business, but the process is streamlined so we can deliver quickly.

Q5Does this include SEO?

It includes SEO-ready structure, homepage copy, a meta title, meta description, headings and local keyword direction. Full SEO campaigns are available separately through Rev Media.

Q6Does this include Google Ads?

No, but Rev Media can set up Google Ads as an upgrade.

Q7Can I request changes?

Yes. One light revision round is included after the first delivery. Larger scope changes can be quoted as an upgrade.

Q8What happens after 24 hours?

You receive the first version for review. From there you can approve it, request light revisions or upgrade into a larger Rev Media package.

Q9How do I pay?

By e-Transfer or credit card. There are no subscriptions and no surprise fees.

Q10Is hosting included?

Ongoing hosting and maintenance are not included by default. We can quote them separately or help you set the site up on your own account.

Q11When does the 24 hour clock start?

We confirm your request within the hour. The 24 hour clock starts as soon as your intake is complete, meaning we have all the required content and details.

Q12Do you work with businesses outside Ontario?

Yes. We are a remote-first studio serving local and international clients.
