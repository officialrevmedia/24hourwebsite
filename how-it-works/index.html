How it works

From intake to launch in 24 hours.

A focused process with no back-and-forth for weeks. Here is what happens, what we need from you and how the guarantee works.

The method

The 24 hour method.

  1. 00:00
    Step 1

    Submit your details

    Tell us what you do, who you serve and what you need. Fill in the intake form or call us. We confirm within the hour and tell you if anything is missing.

  2. 00:30
    Step 2

    We build your foundation

    Our team creates your website structure, starter logo, brand kit and SEO-ready copy. Design and copy happen together, so the logo, colours and words all point the same way.

  3. 22:00
    Step 3

    You review

    We send the first version within 24 hours. You get a link to the site and your brand files. One light revision round is included.

  4. 24:00
    Step 4

    You launch

    You get a professional online presence ready to share, promote and use for leads. We help you connect your domain, or hand over everything you need to launch it yourself.

Before the clock starts

What we need from you.

The 24 hours start once we have everything below. The intake form covers most of it in about two minutes.

  • Your business name
  • Your services and who you serve
  • Contact details for the site
  • Your preferred style, and any sites you like
  • Your logo, if you already have one
  • Any photos or content you want included

The guarantee

24 hours guaranteed or your money back.

Once we receive your completed intake details, we deliver your first version within 24 hours. If we miss the delivery window, you get your money back.

The guarantee applies after all required content and intake information has been submitted. Revision rounds, domain connection delays, third-party platform delays and client response delays are not counted as part of the 24 hour delivery window.
